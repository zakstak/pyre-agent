# Shared internal visual style

The maintained reference is [skills/internal-visual-style/references/BRAND.md](skills/internal-visual-style/references/BRAND.md). Use it for UI and theming work without a specific product design system, including developer plans, review pages, reports, and ad hoc visual artifacts.

The [discoverable skill](skills/internal-visual-style/SKILL.md), [plain HTML example](skills/internal-visual-style/assets/reference.html), and [Tailwind v4 entry](skills/internal-visual-style/assets/tailwind.css) travel together in the complete skill folder. Product-specific branding takes precedence. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono are the chosen reading and technical fonts. The maintained reference records the current screenshot-directed charcoal/violet theme, source/derived color distinction and contrast restrictions; the banner is undecided.
