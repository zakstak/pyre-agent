# Shared internal visual style

The maintained reference is
[skills/internal-visual-style/references/BRAND.md](skills/internal-visual-style/references/BRAND.md).
Use it for UI and theming work that has no product design system, including
developer plans, review pages, reports, and ad hoc visual artifacts. Product
branding takes precedence.

The [skill](skills/internal-visual-style/SKILL.md),
[component specimen](skills/internal-visual-style/assets/reference.html), and
[Tailwind v4 entry](skills/internal-visual-style/assets/tailwind.css) travel
together in the skill folder. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Atkinson
Hyperlegible Mono are the reading and technical fonts. The palette is charcoal
and violet, with exact dark source colors and a derived light companion.
